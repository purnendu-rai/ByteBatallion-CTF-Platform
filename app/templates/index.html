THE BATALLION

> Twelve operators. One standing operation.

Every scoreboard has the same names at the top. In 2026, four operators who kept meeting on those boards stopped competing with each other and started running operations together — that is how the Batallion was born.

A Batallion operation is simple: a target, a scope, and a flag. Every player gets their own flag — no two soldiers carry the same orders, so no shortcut ever works. Solve it yourself, or don't solve it at all.

The Batallion doesn't recruit. It watches the board. Climb it, and the next operation will find you.
